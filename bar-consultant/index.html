FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the difference between a bar consultant and a cocktail consultant?

A cocktail consultant develops the drinks — menus, signature serves, technique. A bar consultant is hired for the operation around those drinks: pour cost, prep and batching, suppliers, inventory, service standards, and the team that has to hit the specs on a Friday. This practice does both; this page is the operations hire.

What does a bar operations consultant actually deliver?

A costed menu, prep workflows the existing team can run, supplier and inventory setup, written specs and SOPs, and on-floor training. The house owns every document when the engagement ends. A list without costings and a trained team is homework, not consulting.

How long does a bar consulting engagement take?

A full opening program runs twelve to sixteen weeks plus ninety days of post-opening optimization. A refresh of a running bar — audit, re-costed menu, retraining — runs six to ten weeks. Advisory retainers are monthly, for rooms that want an outside set of eyes after the handoff.

Do you work with independent bars or only groups?

Both. Independent rooms, restaurant bars, and multi-unit groups. The scope changes with the room — a twenty-seater and a three-unit group do not get the same proposal — but the doctrine does not: costed specs, prep that survives volume, a team that can train the next hire.

How is bar consulting priced?

Fixed project fees scoped before work begins, or a monthly advisory retainer. You know the full cost up front. Honest market ranges are in How Much Does a Bar Consultant Cost?
