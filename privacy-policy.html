Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This site doesn’t use advertising trackers and doesn’t sell data. Here’s what it does handle:

Email. If you write to sani@drinksbyneat.com, your message and address are used only to reply and manage the engagement you’re asking about.

Booking. Calls are scheduled through Calendly, which processes the details you enter under its own privacy policy.

Analytics. Pages load a privacy-friendly Plausible script (data-domain=drinksbyneat.com) to count visits. Plausible does not use cookies or collect personal data for advertising. See their data policy.

Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address when serving font files.

Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may collect standard server logs (IP addresses) for security purposes.

Questions? Get in touch.
